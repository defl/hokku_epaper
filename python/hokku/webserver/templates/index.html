B&W photo pipeline

      

      
Used for black-and-white photos — film scans, old family photos, monochrome art. The server detects these automatically and uses a separate pipeline that avoids the colour-boosting steps which would otherwise add an unwanted pink or yellow tint to the grays.

      

    


    

      
      
    


    

      

        
Face photo pipeline

      

      
Used for photos with people in them. The server detects faces automatically and switches to a pipeline tuned to preserve natural skin tones, which can otherwise shift towards orange or grey on an e-ink display.

      

        
        
      

      

    


    

      

        
Cropping & framing

      

      
Controls how images are fitted to the panel when their aspect ratio doesn't match. A higher fill limit crops more aggressively to remove letterbox bars; face-aware cropping keeps detected faces in frame when that happens.
